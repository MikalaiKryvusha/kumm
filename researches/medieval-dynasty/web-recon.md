## A. The modding scene for Medieval Dynasty

### A.1 Where mods live, and the official stance

- **The Nexus slug is `medievaldynasty`.** The game pages are `https://www.nexusmods.com/medievaldynasty/mods/<id>` and the newer `https://www.nexusmods.com/games/medievaldynasty/mods`. The public Nexus GraphQL API (`api-router.nexusmods.com/graphql`) returns `{"id":3585,"name":"Medieval Dynasty","domainName":"medievaldynasty","modCount":73}`. Queried 2026-10-07. The listing query returned about 66 visible mods. The rest are hidden or removed. (Nexus web pages answer 403 to scripts, so all Nexus data below comes from the API.)
- **There is no Steam Workshop.** `https://steamcommunity.com/app/1129580/workshop/` redirects (302) to the community hub.
- **There is no mod.io page.** None was found: `mod.io/g/medieval-dynasty` serves a generic shell, and web search found no listing. The only "official mod" found is an Opera GX browser skin, which is irrelevant here.
- **No official mod support or modkit exists. It is a "maybe after active development", and the roadmap runs to at least Q1/Q2 2027.** Pinned post "About modding and warfare" by forum moderator *Morri*, Aug 7, 2025: "The game currently does not have official mod support. The devs do not rule this out, but if they implement it, it'll happen only when they are done with active development themselves, and right now they still have a roadmap til at least Q1/Q2 2027." The same post gives the developers' reasons: "For MD it was never originally planned and when they set the scope of the game in the beginning, they never developped it with mod support in mind." It also says: "They are a small team and do not want to deal with the inevitable bug reports … caused by mods". https://steamcommunity.com/app/1129580/discussions/0/591778884098320977/
- **Earlier publisher-side line (same position):** Goldi_Toplitz, May 30, 2022: "It has not been ruled out, but likely won't happen or make much sense during active development." https://steamcommunity.com/app/1129580/discussions/0/3383904768779231445/
- **Moderator warning against save editors:** "Please refrain from using external programs like WeMod or savegame editors yourself … We had countless reports of savegames being ruined that way." (pinned post above, Aug 7, 2025).
- **Upcoming content touches wildlife, not people.** Per PCGH, June 19, 2026: on Sept 22, 2026 a wildlife update adds legendary animals ("Legendäre Tiere ins Spiel bringen"). In Q1–Q2 2027 a new map "Die Hinterwäldler" arrives with a free DLC and a creative mode. https://www.pcgameshardware.de/Medieval-Dynasty-Spiel-72741/News/Grosses-Roadmap-Update-mit-Patch-und-neuer-Map-1545662/ **(summary-fetched)**

### A.3 Loading paths documented to work for this game

- **UE4SS in `Binaries\Win64`** works. Senderos (89) shows this layout: `Win64 ├── Medieval_Dynasty-Win64-Shipping.exe ├── dwmapi.dll ... └── ue4ss\ ... Mods\mods.txt`. Mods are enabled by adding a line such as `SenderosMedievalDynasty : 1` to `ue4ss\Mods\mods.txt`.
- **Engine version override is needed in some builds.** Mods 88 and 89 both instruct `[EngineVersionOverride] MajorVersion = 4, MinorVersion = 27, DebugBuild = false`. https://www.nexusmods.com/medievaldynasty/mods/88
- **The UE4SS build in use is 3.0.1 Beta or an experimental build.** SW_Common (99) says "Tested on Steam build 23681518 with UE4SS v3.0.1 Beta". Shapeshifter (105) bundles "UE4SS v3.0.1-1152-ge3ba1016".
- **Hot reload is turned off by at least one mod.** Shapeshifter install step 5: "Under [General], set: EnableHotReloadSystem = 0 / EnableAutoReloadingLuaMods = 0".
- **Paks in `Content\Paks` are documented, with `-fileopenlog`.** See AIO (77) and Productivity (80). Trajectory Preview drops `MDTrajectoryThread_P.pak` and `MDTrajectoryOutline_P.pak` into `Medieval_Dynasty/Content/Paks` (https://www.nexusmods.com/medievaldynasty/mods/96). MDML's older claim that paks are unsupported because they are signed is contradicted by these 2026 mods.
- **`Content/Paks/~mods` for this game: not found.** No MD mod documents it.
- **`Content/Paks/LogicMods` through BPModLoader for this game: no MD mod documents it.** BPModLoaderMod ships with UE4SS: Senderos' sample `mods.txt` lists `BPModLoaderMod : 1`. In UE4SS v3.0.1 its script reads `Dirs.Game.Content.Paks.LogicMods` and spawns `/Game/Mods/<Name>/ModActor` via `World:SpawnActor(ModClass, {}, {})` inside `ExecuteInGameThread`. https://raw.githubusercontent.com/UE4SS-RE/RE-UE4SS/v3.0.1/assets/Mods/BPModLoaderMod/Scripts/main.lua. The mechanism is generic. Its use on MD is not shown by any public mod.

A bunch of up to 10 people enclosed inside a "village" made of 4 huts and the rest is empty space. Are people afraid to leave their villages? ### B.2 What the developers and moderators said about limits, performance and AI

- **The building cap exists to protect performance, and it scales with NPCs and pathing.** Goldi An Drixta (publisher-side forum staff; quoted in the thread as "GoldiRie"), Oct 17, 2021: "The cap is there to protect you from yourself and us from your anger. More buildings don't just mean more objects, but also more animations, more pathing, more background processes, likely more NPCs. … The limit guarantees a stable performance." https://steamcommunity.com/app/1129580/discussions/0/3112542578517124343/
- **Pathing is the stated worry.** Moderator Morri, Dec 23, 2023: "Sadly 140 is the limit. From what I understood the devs are concerned that more buildings and thus more villagers which will lead to pathing issues that will reflect badly on the game when people start having performance issues." https://steamcommunity.com/app/1129580/discussions/0/4038104598702614760/
- **There will be no raids, because pathfinding AI cannot cope with them.** Pinned post, Aug 7, 2025: "As of now there are no plans to add raids or warfare to the game. MD was never orginally meant to be a combat focused game and the current pathfinding AI wouldn't be able to cope with raids. … It's unlikely to change direction after 6 years." https://steamcommunity.com/app/1129580/discussions/0/591778884098320977/
- **The game is a deliberately peaceful design.** Moderator Morri, Jan 26, 2023: "This is indeed not a full blown survival game but a settlement sim/management game with survival lite and RPG lite elements. It was not intended to have much danger but be mostly a peaceful experience." https://steamcommunity.com/app/1129580/discussions/0/3763355214779460380/
- **Villager use of decorations is planned but slipped.** Moderator Morri, July 2024: "Lack of time. It was supposed to be in the co-op update but didn't make it in time. It will normally be added in the future". https://steamcommunity.com/app/1129580/discussions/0/4416424806096895732/
- **A known NPC-walk bug ("floating").** Player Dragour, Jan 10, 2024: "my villagers might be stuck in specific idle poses and they just float around as if they were walking". https://steamcommunity.com/app/1129580/discussions/4/4038106081463683424/

## C. Industry practice for making a world feel alive

- **S.T.A.L.K.E.R. A-Life: online/offline radius.** This is the closest match to MD's player-proximity activation.
  - **Mechanism.** Dmitriy Iassenev in a Gamasutra interview, Mar 12, 2008: "The offline behavior is very simple: the character does not play animations or sounds, does not manage his inventory actively, does not build detailed and smooth paths (although he does build paths according to the global navigation graph …). In contrast, online behavior is fully detailed. Thus, offline behavior can be considered the LoD (level of detail) of the online." On the radius: "only those who are in a predefined radius from the player (can vary from level to level, but usually it is about 150 meters), or what the game designer sets it to." https://www.gamedeveloper.com/pc/interview-inside-the-ai-of-i-s-t-a-l-k-e-r-i-
  - **Pitfall (2024).** When the offline layer is missing, the world dies outside a small bubble. S.T.A.L.K.E.R. 2 at launch, GamingBolt, Dec 4, 2024, quoting modder ConnerRia: "The offline A-Life of the past games no longer exists. … Except this kind of flat out does not work." The vision radius was "quite low (about 80 meters)". GSC CEO: "To work properly, this system requires a much larger area for spawn NPCs and much more memory resources. We were fighting with optimization." https://gamingbolt.com/s-t-a-l-k-e-r-2-heart-of-chornobyls-a-life-2-0-isnt-working-heres-why
- **Oblivion Radiant AI.**
  - **Mechanism.** NPCs get goals and a schedule and choose how to reach them. Wikipedia: "General goals, such as 'Eat in this location at 2pm' are given to NPCs, and NPCs are left to determine how to achieve them." https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radiant_AI **(summary-fetched)**
  - **Pitfall.** Autonomous NPCs broke authored quests, so the system was toned down. Emil Pagliarulo, quoted by GamesBeat (Dec 17, 2010): "Designers need a certain amount of control over the scenarios they create, and things can go haywire when NPCs have a mind of their own. … NPCs from the local skooma den were trying to get their fix, didn't have any skooma, and were killing the merchant to get it!" Also: "we the developers have had to consciously tone down the types of behavior they carry out." https://gamesbeat.com/dimming-the-radiant-ai-in-oblivion/
  - **Lesson.** Good simulation is invisible. Bruce Nesmith, FRVR, Oct 16, 2025: "The quirkiness of Radiant AI in Oblivion is what makes it noticeable." The article calls it an "enormous" investment. https://frvr.com/blog/news/elder-scrolls-designer-explains-that-radiant-ai-is-an-enormous-investment-for-bethesda-but-every-improvement-actually-makes-it-less-noticeable-to-players/
- **Skyrim Radiant Story.**
  - **Mechanism.** Data tags on NPCs and locations (relationship, dungeon type) let generic quests fill their slots dynamically. Wikipedia: "Dynamically generated quests are placed by the game in locations the player hasn't visited yet and are related to earlier adventures." **(summary-fetched)**
  - **Pitfalls.** GamesBeat, Jan 27, 2012: "Job quests were limited to certain NPCs that felt right for them, like innkeepers, to avoid Radiant quests feeling mechanical." Nesmith: "When every time I drop something on the ground, half the town comes over, it is starting to get really silly". And: "We ended up doing many more custom pieces. It felt better." Also: "If we pass the buck to a system, it's not the same." https://gamesbeat.com/bethesdas-nesmith-reflects-on-the-difficult-birth-of-skyrims-radiant-story-system/
  - **Later anti-pattern.** Fallout 4's endless "Another settlement needs your help" quests became a meme for repetitive radiant quests. https://amp.knowyourmeme.com/memes/another-settlement-needs-our-help (secondary source)
- **RimWorld AI storyteller (Tynan Sylvester).**
  - **Mechanism.** Events are chosen by a pacing director, not at random. "RimWorld uses an "AI storyteller" (modeled after the AI Director from Left 4 Dead) who analyzes your situation and decides which event she thinks will make the best story." https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/About_RimWorld
  - **Pacing.** "Cassandra creates story events on a classic increasing curve of challenge and tension. She'll push you with dangerous events, then give breathing room, then come back to push once more." https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/AI_Storytellers **(summary-fetched)**
  - **Core lesson: the Player Model principle.** Sylvester, "The Simulation Dream", June 2, 2013: "anything in the Game Model that doesn't copy into the Player Model is worthless." Also: "We can't blindly simulate everything, because most things are boring and people can't understand over-complex systems anyway." And: "This apophenia … is the key to making a simulation game work."
  - **Anti-pattern he cites.** Richard Garriott on Ultima Online's ecology: "all the players went in and just killed everything; so fast that the game couldn't spawn them fast enough to make the simulation even begin. … literally no-one ever noticed – ever – and we eventually just ripped it out of the game." https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-simulation-dream
- **Kenshi.**
  - **Mechanism.** World states react to the deaths of notable NPCs. Wikipedia: "The game includes a world states system which creates reactions to deaths of notable people." Also: "These reactions to power vacuums can result in new locations spawning or in towns being taken over by new factions." https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenshi_(video_game) **(summary-fetched)**
  - **Effect.** The Register, May 31, 2019: "the world of Kenshi goes on with or without the player's input. Factions wage war, merchant caravans crawl across the wastes and cannibals eat each other." https://www.theregister.com/2019/05/31/kenshi_sandblasted_swordpunk_where_the_dungeon_master_wants_everyone_dead/
  - **Pitfalls.** Not found from an authoritative developer source.
- **Mount & Blade II: Bannerlord campaign AI.**
  - **Mechanism.** Parties on the map follow the player's rules and score their options mostly by distance. TaleWorlds dev blog via GameBanshee, Dec 23, 2019: "in Bannerlord, NPC lords are bound by many of the same restrictions and rules as the player." On sieges, one factor is proximity, "which should help reduce the chances of those crazy expeditions deep into enemy territory". https://www.gamebanshee.com/3wfc8
  - **Pitfall: a snowballing simulation.** Patch e1.0.4 (Apr 4, 2020): "some lords were remaining without troops in their party because of financial problems and constantly being harassed by bandits. … This was one reason for the snowball effect in the campaign, with kingdoms being eliminated too easily." https://www.thesixthaxis.com/2020/04/04/mount-blade-2-bannerlord-crash-fix-update-1-0-4/ Patch e1.0.5 added "Snowball effect developments", e.g. "AI defection calculation now cares about relations much more". https://www.thesixthaxis.com/2020/04/06/mount-blade-2-bannerlord-crash-fix-update-1-0-5/ **(summary-fetched)**
- **Dwarf Fortress.**
  - **Mechanism.** Stack Overflow blog: "The simulation runs deep, with new games creating multiple civilizations with histories, mythologies, and artifacts."
  - **Pitfall 1: emergent bugs from interacting systems.** Tarn Adams: "the cats were showing up dead all over the tavern floor, and it turned out they were ingesting spilled alcohol when they cleaned their paws. One number was off in the ingest-while-cleaning code".
  - **Pitfall 2: pathfinding is the scaling wall.** Adams: "We are certainly at the edge of what we can currently support in terms of agents and map complexity". Their fix: "keep track of connected components reachable by walking … if the component numbers are the same, they know the call will succeed." https://stackoverflow.blog/2021/07/28/700000-lines-of-code-20-years-and-one-developer-how-dwarf-fortress-is-built/ (republished Dec 31, 2021)
- **Shadow of Mordor Nemesis.**
  - **Mechanism.** A persistent hierarchy of named orcs whose power struggles resolve over time.
  - **Pitfall: feature creep away from the core.** Michael de Plater, quoted by Kotaku, Jan 22, 2015: "it went through quite a bit of feature creep which made it significantly more complex and took it further away from the core promise. For example at one point we had multiple Uruk Factions with separate bars for Morale and Discipline". https://kotaku.com/shadow-of-mordors-nemesis-system-couldve-been-way-more-1681120649
- **Kingdom Come: Deliverance 1 and 2: persistent NPC day cycles.** Martin Labuť, GDS 2024 talk "NPC Daycycles In Kingdom Come: Deliverance 2": "nearly 2400 persistent NPCs follow their daily schedules at all times. … This not only challenges the game's performance but also requires a nontrivial amount of manual labor to place all these NPCs in the game. Furthermore, many NPCs are featured in various quests, which interact with their daycycles." https://2024.gdsession.com/index.php?action=kcd. Follow-up GDC session (news, Sept 3, 2026): "keeps thousands of NPCs on independent schedules and paths, even when the player never meets them". https://gamedev.net/news/5526-supporting-thousands-of-npcs-in-kingdom-come-deliverance-kingdom-come/
- **AI level of detail, done properly (Game AI Pro, free chapters).**
  - **Distance LOD is a hack.** Ben Sunshine-Hill, "Phenomenal AI Level-of-Detail Control with the LOD Trader" (Game AI Pro, 2013). He describes the usual approach: "simulate out-of-view characters at a lower update rate, or … delete characters entirely when they're too far away … our conscience whispers, "That's just a hack … someone's going to notice.""
  - **Taxonomy of what players catch.** "A character disappearing while momentarily around a corner, or having been frozen in place for hours while the player was away … creates the potential for a fundamental discontinuity BIR". And: "A character wandering randomly instead of having goal-driven behaviors is the most common example of an unrealistic long-term behavior BIR". http://www.gameaipro.com/GameAIPro/GameAIPro_Chapter14_Phenomenal_AI_Level-of-Detail_Control_with_the_LOD_Trader.pdf
  - **Background NPCs need cheap schedules.** David "Rez" Graham, "Breathing Life into Your Background Characters" (Game AI Pro, used on The Sims Medieval): "If you have static NPCs that don't look like they're busy with their lives, your world will feel stale." On anti-patterns: looping idles and random walks; "neither of these techniques looks very good". Also: "Another common pitfall is to swing the pendulum in the other direction and attempt to implement a deep, rich AI system." And: "Only seeing four or five NPCs wandering around is not enough to convince the player that he's in a city, but seeing 20 or 30 might be." http://www.gameaipro.com/GameAIPro/GameAIPro_Chapter36_Breathing_Life_into_Your_Background_Characters.pdf
  - **Planners lose to authored daily scripts.** Robert Zubek, "1000 NPCs at 60 FPS" (Game AI Pro 3, Project Highrise): "by employing planning, we were working on the wrong level of abstraction … what we actually wanted to do, was to author peoples' entire daily routines at a high level". On their simulation: "we needed the NPCs to be simulated on an ongoing basis instead of being simply instanced into view and then culled." http://www.gameaipro.com/GameAIPro3/GameAIPro3_Chapter34_1000_NPCs_at_60_FPS.pdf

## D. UE4 and UE4SS techniques for adding life

- **Spawning actors from Lua.** In UE4SS docs (main branch), `UWorld:SpawnActor(UClass ActorClass, FVector Location, FRotator Rotation)`: "This function uses `UGameplayStatics:BeginDeferredActorSpawnFromClass` and `UGameplayStatics:FinishSpawningActor` to spawn an actor." https://github.com/UE4SS-RE/RE-UE4SS/blob/main/docs/lua-api/classes/uworld.md
  - This page does **not** exist at tag v3.0.1 (404). The 3.0.1 BPModLoader script nevertheless calls `World:SpawnActor(ModClass, {}, {})` inside `ExecuteInGameThread`, so the method is present in 3.0.1. Note that the main docs header says "For version: **4.0.0**".
- **`StaticConstructObject`** "mimics the function StaticConstructObject_Internal" and creates a UObject (not a placed actor). https://github.com/UE4SS-RE/RE-UE4SS/blob/main/docs/lua-api/global-functions/staticconstructobject.md
- **Known spawning crash.** Issue #527 (open, May 25, 2024, v3.0.1, UE5.1 game): "Calling `UWorld::SpawnActor()` in a game thread from both Lua and C++ leads to crashes after 3-10 actors spawned on average." "calling `CheatManager::summon()` from the UE console **with the same actor class** allows to spawn infinitely many actors". https://github.com/UE4SS-RE/RE-UE4SS/issues/527
- **Game thread.** "`ExecuteInGameThread` is a function that executes code on the game thread using either the ProcessEvent hook or the EngineTick hook." The docs also say `LoadAsset` "Must only be called from within the game thread." https://github.com/UE4SS-RE/RE-UE4SS/blob/main/docs/lua-api/global-functions/executeingamethread.md and https://github.com/UE4SS-RE/RE-UE4SS/blob/main/docs/lua-api.md
- **Async APIs are unsafe.** Issue #168 (open since July 26, 2023): "Currently async APIs will execute the same lua_state in a separate OS thread. … All async APIs will eventually crash because of this." It adds: "registerhook something frequent and LoopAsync, RegisterHotkeyAsync or ExecuteDeferred with a low delay. You will eventually crash." https://github.com/UE4SS-RE/RE-UE4SS/issues/168
  - The newer API deprecates `LoopAsync`/`ExecuteAsync` in favour of `LoopInGameThreadWithDelay`/`ExecuteInGameThreadWithDelay`. The upgrade guide says: "New system has better thread safety, particularly when interacting with game objects". This exists only in post-3.0.1 builds. https://github.com/UE4SS-RE/RE-UE4SS/blob/main/docs/upgrade-guide.md
- **Overlapping deferred callbacks crash.** Issue #1180 (closed Feb 2026, v3.0.1-932): "When multiple deferred actions overlap via `ExecuteInGameThread` … the game crashes with `get_function_ref` "Ref was not function"". https://github.com/UE4SS-RE/RE-UE4SS/issues/1180
- **Hot reload and mod restarts are risky.** Issue #1445 (open, Sept 28, 2026): "A Lua mod that restarts (`RestartCurrentMod`, Restart All Mods) can deadlock the game, corrupt the heap, or corrupt its own Lua state". https://github.com/UE4SS-RE/RE-UE4SS/issues/1445
  - Issue #1345 (open, July 17, 2026): "Lua runs concurrently from the async thread and the game thread". https://github.com/UE4SS-RE/RE-UE4SS/issues/1345
  - Shared variables "do not get reset when hot-reloading" (Mod class docs). https://github.com/UE4SS-RE/RE-UE4SS/blob/main/docs/lua-api/classes/mod.md
  - At least one MD mod (Shapeshifter) tells users to set `EnableHotReloadSystem = 0`.
- **Hooks.** "Any `UFunction` that you attempt to register with `RegisterHook` must already exist in memory when you register it." Also: "`RegisterHook` doesn't support delegate functions!" For non-`/Script/` paths (i.e. Blueprint functions) the callback runs **after** the function executes. https://github.com/UE4SS-RE/RE-UE4SS/blob/main/docs/lua-api/global-functions/registerhook.md
- **Keeping mod state across save/load.** UE4SS has **no dedicated persistence API** in its docs (not found). The Lua API is offered "on top of the standard libraries that Lua comes with" (lua-api.md), so `io` file writes are the de-facto route. For example, Anywhere Fast Travel (MD mod 88): "Your Home location is saved to disk so it remains available the next time you play." No documented pattern was found for tying a sidecar file to a specific MD save slot. Issue #602 asks for an `on_hot_reload` hook (open since 2024).
- **UE4 actor and character cost limits.**
  - **Animation Budget Allocator (UE 4.27 plugin).** It "determines a fixed budget … in milliseconds of work to perform on the game thread" and throttles skeletal-mesh ticking by significance. Setup requires changing the mesh component class to `SkeletalMeshComponentBudgeted`. https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/animation-budget-allocator-in-unreal-engine **(summary-fetched)**
  - **Forum consensus: CharacterMovement is the main cost.** User Bariudol, July 10, 2019 (not Epic staff): "the biggest obstacle to have lots and lots of characters in one world is the movement component, which is pretty expensive" and "you can have a couple of hundreds without issues" with simple AI. https://forums.unrealengine.com/t/how-many-pawns-with-ai-controllers-is-too-many-in-one-level-at-one-time/451987 **(summary-fetched)**

## Top 10 implications for a living-world mod of Medieval Dynasty — SYNTHESIS (my inference, not sourced fact)

1. **Build a two-tier simulation (A-Life pattern).** Run an abstract, data-only "offline" world (travellers, caravans, bandit bands and village events as records moving along the road graph on a game-time tick). Materialise real actors only inside a radius of the player. Players report that MD already animates other villages only near the player, so the offline layer is what is missing.
2. **Respect pathfinding.** The developers explicitly say the "pathfinding AI wouldn't be able to cope with raids", and the building cap exists for "more pathing". Keep materialised NPCs few (tens, not hundreds). Prefer road-following or scripted movement over open navmesh pathing, and despawn them on leaving the radius.
3. **Hide discontinuities.** The LOD Trader taxonomy predicts the obvious failures: people popping out of existence, being frozen while you were away, wandering aimlessly. Spawn travellers beyond view distance. Give every abstract agent a destination and a purpose so its state on materialisation matches elapsed time.
4. **Pick events with a pacing director, not raw randomness.** Use a RimWorld-style director that reads world state (season, village wealth from BP_EconomyManager, reputation) and alternates tension and breathing room. Keep events opt-in or tunable: forum voices insist danger must stay optional ("All optional, of course!").
5. **Make every simulated thing visible (Sylvester's Player Model principle).** A caravan nobody meets, or a price shift nobody notices, is worthless, as UO's ecology was. Surface the off-screen world through rumours, notices, prices and visitors, not only through hidden numbers.
6. **Keep authored hooks under designer control.** Radiant AI broke quests when NPCs acted freely. Never let new autonomous agents touch quest-critical NPCs or items that BP_QuestManager owns. Whitelist the actors the mod may move or kill.
7. **Use templates plus tags for random quests (Radiant Story), with repetition caps.** Bethesda had to limit job quests to fitting NPCs and add frequency limits. Fallout 4's endless "settlement needs help" shows what happens without them.
8. **Use schedules, not planners, for background villagers.** Graham and Zubek both converge on cheap, data-driven daily routines. That fits Blueprint plus DataTables: extend the existing day cycle with routine entries (decoration use, visits, market days) rather than a new AI brain.
9. **Program UE4SS defensively.** Do all world mutation inside `ExecuteInGameThread`. Avoid `LoopAsync`. Rate-limit spawns: issue #527 reports crashes after a few `SpawnActor` calls, so test early whether MD has the same problem. Prefer reusing or pooling NPCs, or calling the game's own spawn functions on its managers, over raw spawns. Disable hot reload in shipped builds.
10. **Persist mod state yourself.** Write a sidecar file per save, keyed on the save slot or name, with `io`. Hook the game's save and load functions to flush and restore it. Version the format, because MD saves break across updates according to the moderators, and a mod-side file is safer than editing the save.
