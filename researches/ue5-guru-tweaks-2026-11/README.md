## 0. Bottom line

1. **The guru the owner means is P40L0** (Nexus; Patreon "techoptimized"). He makes "Ultimate Engine Tweaks" (UET): one big Engine.ini, published per game for 60+ UE4/UE5 games. Since 2026-10-02 there is also a universal version, v137 (https://www.nexusmods.com/site/mods/2349). He claims "over 1.7 million downloads with 60+ Unreal Engine 5 and 4 games" (his description, same page). He has **no Conan Exiles version** (search 2026-11-09). The universal UET is the closest to our engine: its explanation sheet compares against stock UE 5.7.
   - Two other prolific authors exist, both near ≈2.0 M downloads by the Nexus API (2026-11-09). VynnGfx publishes "Optimized Tweaks" for 18 games, but ships them as `.pak` files, so the lines cannot be read from the page. SammiLucia, with Lazorr and StoneyDays, makes Ultra+ for 14 mods; recent Ultra+ versions are native code fixes, not ini lines.
   - Neither fits "Engine.ini for very many games" as well as P40L0.
2. **Most of P40L0's file does nothing in Conan 3.0.1.** The universal UET has 505 unique names.
   - **187 are registered in 3.0.1** and **318 are not** (242 non-log names plus 75 log-category lines plus one `Global` line).
   - Not registered includes 76 of the 84 lines in his "Parallel computing" block (`r.ParallelCulling`, `r.ParallelPhysicsStepAsync`, `r.ParallelAnimationRetargeting`, …). Nine of these names, spot-checked, have no page in the UE 5.4.4 source wiki either (HTTP 404, while an existing page returns 200). They look invented, not just missing from 5.8.
   - Of the 187 registered lines, most are engine defaults already, are already set by Funcom, or change the look (§2, §3).
   - His per-line explanations and all his numbers come from AI, not from measurement. The sheet is titled "Copilot Pro (GPT-5) … Full Claude Analysis (Sonnet 5 Max)", and the 31-page analysis says: «All figures in Sections 6 and 7 are estimates, not lab measurements».
3. **Independent A-B of UET: no difference.** Digital Foundry (Alex Battaglia) A-B tested UET in Oblivion Remastered: «it runs the same»; run-to-run differences were «simple variance» (VideoGamer, last updated May 6 2025: https://www.videogamer.com/news/oblivion-remastered-most-popular-performance-mod-is-actually-all-placebo). Our own Palworld audit of P40L0 v12 found the same: 764 of 1173 assignments set a cvar the build does not have (`researches/ue5-cvars/README.md` rule 1; `D:/work/ai_sandbox/Palworld/_config/Pareto-audit.md`).
4. **Still, a few of his registered lines are worth a run. None of them is in our earlier research as a candidate.** Each is a no-look-change fix for stutter that real play meets: `niagara.CreateShadersOnLoad=1` (effect shaders created at load, not at first use), `r.CreateShadersOnLoad=1` (from the r/OptimizedGaming "universal stutter fix", the same idea for all shaders) and Cascade particle batching (`FX.BatchAsync=1`, `FX.EarlyScheduleAsync=1`). Ultra+ and LTSharpe both measured the stalls of freeing and re-creating GPU upload memory. Ultra+ (Hogwarts Legacy, UE 4.27, its own numbers) reports «about 220 times on a 76 s flight, up to 28 ms each». Their fix has a partial cvar handle in 5.8: `d3d12.FastAllocator.MinPagesToRetain`.
5. **New facts about Conan from its own log**, not in earlier notes. They close several popular "stutter fixes" before any run:
   - The bundled PSO cache (`ConanSandbox_PCD3D_SM6.stable.upipelinecache`, 3568 PSOs) completes at startup «in 0.70s».
   - Funcom already sets the UE 5.x PSO precaching lines (`r.PSOPrecache.ProxyCreationStrategy 2`, `GlobalShaders 1`, `D3D12.DriverCacheAware 1`, `KeepInMemoryForActiveMaterials 1`).
   - The engine's old D3D12 PSO disk cache is off («Not using pipeline state disk cache per r.D3D12.PSO.DiskCache=0»), and Epic's own source says it «doesn't work anymore».
   - Nanite runs on reserved resources (`r.Nanite.Streaming.ReservedResources 1`). This probably removes the root-page "grow by copy" freeze that `r.Nanite.Streaming.NumInitialRootPages` targets (batch q8).

## 4. Suggested order of runs

1. **A1** `niagara.CreateShadersOnLoad=1`, then **A2** `r.CreateShadersOnLoad=1`. Both are ini lines before launch, A-B over launches. They need a **first-use** scene, which a warm flight cannot show: a fresh launch, then the first minute of combat and effects, plus the cold first flight. Record RAM/VRAM and the time to enter the world.
2. **A3** `d3d12.FastAllocator.MinPagesToRetain=32`: warm flights, metric hitches ≥ 50 ms and the worst frame.
3. **A4**, then **A5** (Cascade batching), as live `exec`: median `MsCPUBusy` in a busy camp at night.
4. **A6**, **B1**, **B2** only after the WPR thread split (`conan-main-thread` §2A) shows where RT waits.
5. The queued earlier candidates (`conan-engine-ini-cpu` §3, `bench-batches/README.md`) keep their place. P40L0 adds nothing that outranks them.

## 5. Gaps

- Whether Conan's effects are Cascade or Niagara (A4/A5 vs A1) is unknown. A `dumpticks` in a camp (`conan-main-thread` §2B) would show which.
- The Conan 3.0.1 values of A1, A2, A3, A4, A5 and B1 were not read in game; the defaults above come from 5.4.4 source.
- VynnGfx's `.pak` contents and Ultra+ Oblivion's cvar set were not read (packaged). Unpacking them could show lines that this file has not checked.
- The Digital Foundry test is known through a news report, not the video. Epic's Hitch Hunt talk is known through a news report, not the slides.
