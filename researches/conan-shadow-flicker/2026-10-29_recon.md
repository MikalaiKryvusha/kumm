## Findings: why the September levers could not work

1. **The sun's light rotates almost every frame. Every rotation throws away the whole sun shadow cache.** Epic docs: «Any light movement or
   rotation will invalidate all cached pages for that light». Funcom help text from the 3.0.1 exe: `DynamicSky.SolarUpdateAngleDelta` = «How
   many degrees can the sun/moon move in the sky before we rotate the corresponding directional light to match». A default day is about 30 real
   minutes (Funcom forum), so the sun moves about 0.15–0.2°/s. At 0.003° that is a light rotation about 50 times a second, roughly every frame at
   70 fps. The game's own 0.015° gives about 10 rotations a second.
   *Inference (my reasoning, not a quote):* the shadow texel grid is anchored in light space, so a rotation by Δθ moves the grid against a point
   at distance R from the world origin by R·Δθ. For R ≈ 2 km and 0.003° that is about 10 cm, many texels at the finest level. Every rotation
   samples the thin palm fronds again at a new sub-texel phase, and the aliasing pattern of the frond shadows rolls fresh each time. That reads as
   flicker on top of the wind sway. Cascaded shadow maps behave the same way when the light rotates, so turning VSM off would NOT remove this
   cause.
2. **This explains all three failed levers.** While the clipmap is invalidated every frame by the light, nothing that touches the cache can
   calm it: not WPO invalidation, not caching, not coarse pages.
   - `r.Shadow.Virtual.Cache.DeformableMeshesInvalidate=0` was aimed at the wrong target anyway. Its help text in the 3.0.1 exe: «Primitive
     Proxies that are marked as having deformable meshes (HasDeformableMesh() == true) cause invalidations…». That covers deformable meshes
     (skinned and similar), not static foliage with wind WPO.
   - `ResolutionLodBiasDirectionalMoving` does not mean "blur moving objects". Help text: «Bias applied to LOD calculations for directional
     **lights that are moving** … transitions smoothly back to ResolutionLodBiasDirectional as the light transitions to non-moving». Conan's sun
     never stops, so this is the bias actually in use. Setting it to 2.0 (from -1.0) cut shadow resolution about 8×, which is why the owner said
     «качество теней ужасное просто».
   - SMRT 16/16 only softens the penumbra. It cannot stop the pattern from changing every frame.
3. **UE 5.7+ default change.** `r.Shadow.Virtual.UseReceiverMaskDirectional` is now 1 by default. Epic support (Lance Chaney): «This is the
   intended default behavior in 5.7 … receiver mask will be used for all dynamic pages», so dynamic pages stay uncached. This costs performance,
   but the thread mentions no flicker.
4. **Conan-specific reports.** Funcom keeps an open PC bug list. Its Pt. 2 lists «Flickering shadows to light and back — since May 2026» and
   «Distorted geometric shadows», with no fix. One player report (Aug 2026) found a partial workaround: «General Shadows Low» + «Lumen GI Low».
   No Nexus mod or Funcom fix for foliage shadow flicker was found.
5. **No WPO-range cvar exists in this build.** `r.Shadow.Virtual.Cache.MaxMaterialPositionInvalidationRange` and `…Cache.StaticSeparate` are
   absent. The per-asset fix Epic documents («Shadow Cache Invalidation Behavior = Rigid»: «Suppress invalidations that would otherwise be
   generated by, for example, World Position Offset») needs the Dev Kit and a foliage-asset mod. It is also pointless while the light rotates.

## Test plan (agent runs it; the owner's eye on the 4K TV judges)

**Stand (once per session), so that the frame repeats:**
- Take the machine lock (`_tools/machine-lock.mjs`). Launch with `launch-bg.ps1`, without focus, and send all commands through the pult
  (`bash ConanExiles/_config/pult.sh "<cmd>"`). Apply cvars **live** with `exec <cvar> <value>` and read each one back by sending its name with
  no value. No restarts between A and B, so the sun and the camera cannot drift between runs (the September lesson).
- Freeze the conditions: `exec God`, thirst and hunger off (internal map). Put the hero under the same palms with `p:K2_SetActorLocation(<fixed
  point>, …)`. The camera pose must be **absolute**: a `CameraActor` + `SetViewTarget`, as `KrinikConanBench/Scripts/main.lua` does. Never use
  the relative `input.ps1 -Look`.
- Set the time of day before each pair: `FindFirstOf('BP_TimeOfDayManager_C'):SetTimeOfDay(13.0)` (the console `SetTimeOfDay` does not exist in
  3.0.1).
- Each run is **A–B–A**: base, then the lever, then base again, about 10 s apart. Capture `capture-burst.ps1 -Count 40 -IntervalMs 25 -Width 3840`
  for each, then `frame-diff.py` on a fixed box of sand under the fronds: d2/d1 ≈ 1 means flicker, ≈ 2 means sway. Compare only within one A–B–A.
  The owner gets a 4K clip in his own review folder. The verdict is his eye.
